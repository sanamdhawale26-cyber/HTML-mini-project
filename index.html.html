The Midnight Gallery Mystery

A priceless painting called "The Golden Moon" has disappeared from the Midnight Art Gallery. The painting was last seen inside the main exhibition hall shortly before midnight.

What Happened?

On Monday night, the gallery closed to visitors at 10:00 PM. When the security guard checked the exhibition hall at midnight, the painting was missing from its frame.

The doors appeared to be locked, and no broken windows were reported.

Known Facts

Fact 1: The painting was present at 9:45 PM.
Fact 2: The gallery closed at 10:00 PM.
Fact 3: The alarm showed an unusual interruption at 11:30 PM.

Your Mission

You are the detective assigned to this case. Study the known facts, think about the timeline, and identify what might have happened to the missing painting.
