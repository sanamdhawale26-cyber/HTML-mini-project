Suspect One: Alex Morgan

Background: Alex manages the gallery's exhibitions and records.

Possible motive: Alex was worried about the gallery's financial problems.

Reported alibi: Alex says they were checking inventory in the office at 11:30 PM.

Additional Details

  • Access to gallery records
    • Exhibition schedule
    • Inventory register
  • Interview status: Pending

Important: Alex could access the gallery records.

Note: Access alone does not prove involvement.

Suspect Two: Bella Carter

Background: Bella is a collector who recently visited the gallery.

Possible motive: Bella had expressed strong interest in the Golden Moon painting.

Reported alibi: Bella says she left the building at 9:50 PM.

A fictional witness stated: I saw Bella near the main exhibition hall.

This statement is a clue to verify, not proof of guilt.

Earlier report: The visitor left at 9:30 PM.

Corrected report: The visitor left at 9:50 PM.

Case closed Investigation remains open.

Suspect Three: Daniel Reed

Background: Daniel repairs and restores old paintings.

Possible motive: Daniel wanted professional recognition for his work.

Reported alibi: Daniel says he was in the restoration workshop during the late evening.

The painting's label contained a reference to CCTV footage near the workshop.

"I was working in the restoration room that night. Please check the workshop log."
- Daniel Reed, fictional interview statement

Evidence code: WORKSHOP-03 

Workshop log:
Time: 11:30 PM
Entry: To be verified

Archive record: The painting was catalogued as Golden Moon.

Investigation Next Steps

  1. Review the gallery security logs.
  2. Verify each suspect's reported alibi.
  3. Interview witnesses and record their statements.
  4. Compare the timeline with available evidence.

Investigation Terms

Motive
A possible reason someone might do something.
Alibi
An account of where a person was at a particular time.
Witness
A person who may have seen or heard something relevant.
